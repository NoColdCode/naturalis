# Naturalis - Mod Overview

Naturalis is a survival-focused morph expansion that turns shapeshifting into a full progression system.

Instead of morphs being only cosmetic, each form has identity, strengths, instincts, and long-term mastery. You learn each body over time, unlock better control, and eventually bond with one mastered morph through Resonance.

## Core fantasy

- Become creatures and actually feel different while playing.
- Learn your current form through use, movement, and survival.
- Trade convenience for immersion in early levels, then earn mastery.
- Build a bonded endgame identity with Resonance.

## Main gameplay pillars

- Morph XP progression per morph (`0` to `2000`) with branch-based mastery
- Instinct behaviors (hunt, fear, wander, smell hints)
- Diet compatibility (carnivore, herbivore, omnivore)
- Body inertia and metabolism by morph mass
- Quadruped inventory and hotbar restrictions that ease with mastery
- Resonance bonding, humanity management, and active instinct combat windows
- Morph-themed vision filters and sensory cues

## Player loop

1. Acquire a morph.
2. Stay in that form and play normally to gain Knowledge XP.
3. Spend knowledge points in branch ranks (vitality, handling, instinct, wander, etc.).
4. Reach resonance eligibility for a morph (`1400 XP`) and bond it.
5. Manage humanity, use active instinct windows, and specialize your combat identity.

## Current Morph Knowledge model (1.4.x)

Morph progression is now best understood as XP + branch ranks, not only a simple level ladder.

- Per-morph XP range: `0` to `2000`
- Legacy display levels still exist (`0` to `5`) as milestones:
	- Level 1: `180 XP`
	- Level 2: `360 XP`
	- Level 3: `720 XP`
	- Level 4: `1280 XP`
	- Level 5: `2000 XP`
- Practical progression power comes from branch ranks and point spending.

Main branches include:

- Vitality
- Handling
- Instinct
- Wander
- Resonance (human connection)
- Damage
- Morph Resistance
- Utilities
- Social

Resonance eligibility is based on morph XP:

- A morph becomes bond-eligible at `1400 XP`

## What makes Naturalis different

- Progression is tied to behavior, not just items.
- Different bodies create different constraints, not only bonuses.
- Mastery gives back control over time.
- Endgame focuses on identity alignment, not raw stat inflation.
