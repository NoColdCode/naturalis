## Naturalis

**Survival morph progression for modern Minecraft.** Naturalis turns ReMorphed’s shapeshifting into a full identity loop — learn each body, master its instincts, and bond with one form through **Resonance** while managing your **Humanity**.

Naturalis is built on **ReMorphed**, **Walkers**, and **CraftedCore**. It does not replace morph unlocking; it adds depth on top: per-morph Knowledge XP, branch-based mastery, body-specific constraints, sensory systems, and endgame bonding with real risk.

## Getting Started

1. **Install dependencies** — CraftedCore, Walkers, and ReMorphed (see Requirements below).
2. **Hunt & unlock** — use ReMorphed as usual: kill mobs to add them to your collection.
3. **Live in a form** — stay morphed and play normally to earn **Morph Knowledge** XP for that body.
4. **Spend ranks** — open the Morph Knowledge screen to invest in branches (handling, instinct, vitality, etc.).
5. **Bond with Resonance** — at **1400 XP** on a morph, you can bond it for endgame bonuses — but watch your **Humanity**.
6. **Quick morph** — **hold G** for the radial quick-slot wheel; **tap G** to toggle human ↔ current morph (ReMorphed transform key).

⚠️ **Note:** Naturalis extends ReMorphed and Walkers. You need all three dependency mods installed. ReMorphed’s unlock flow stays; Naturalis adds progression, instincts, and Resonance on top.

## Morph Knowledge

Each unlocked morph has its own XP track (**0–2000**) and branch ranks. Early levels are restrictive — smaller hotbars, inventory limits, stronger instinct nudges — and mastery gives control back. Plan a main morph, specialize branches, and read the in-game Knowledge screen often.

**[Knowledge — branch / XP screen screenshot]**

## Resonance & Humanity

Pick **one bonded morph** as your advanced identity. While aligned, you gain mastery tiers and active **Instinct** combat windows — but Humanity drifts down over time. Recover in human form, or push into Feral stages for shorter cooldowns at higher risk. At **0 Humanity**, you are **Lost**.

**[Resonance — humanity / bond UI screenshot]**

## Instinct & body feel

Passive instincts (hunt, fear, wander, smell hints) respond to your Instinct rank. With Resonance active, trigger **Active Instinct** on cooldown for a combat burst whose timing depends on Humanity and mastery. Diet, inertia, metabolism, quadruped handling, natural attacks, and morph-themed vision all change how each body plays.
