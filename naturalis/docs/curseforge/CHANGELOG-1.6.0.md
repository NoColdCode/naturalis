## Naturalis 1.6.0 — Embodiment & quick morph

**Realistic survival morphs, scent as a sense, and a radial quick-slot wheel.**

### New

- **Experience modes** — Realistic (full head/camera embodiment) or Softened (comfort-oriented; keeps vision & scent).
- **Morph head movement** — archetype gait sway, neck limits, dig-view pull, and first-person body feedback in Realistic mode.
- **Scent vision** — active sniff briefly highlights the world through a scent overlay.
- **Scent ribbons** — continuous trail ribbons for prey, hostiles, and deep-sniff targets.
- **Quick-slot morph wheel** — hold **G** for the radial menu, release to morph; tap **G** to toggle human ↔ morph.
- **Species secondary actions (WIP)** — Sniff, Listen, and Peck on supported morphs (more coming).

### Fixes

- Forge jar build (duplicate mixin refmap).
- Quick-slot morph selection and transform-key conflicts on Forge.

### Note

Secondary species mechanics and some ribbon/embodiment tuning are still **work in progress** — feedback welcome.

**Requires:** CraftedCore, Walkers, ReMorphed.
