Paste into the **Changelog** field on your CurseForge file upload.

## Naturalis 1.6.3 — Mod integrations, Survival as…, morph stability

**Hundreds of bundled mob profiles, Survival as… as a real Create World mode, richer natural attacks, and morph stability fixes.**

### New

- **Expanded bundled mob profiles** — Naturalist, Aquamirae, L_Ender's Cataclysm, Friends & Foes, Critters and Companions, Twilight Forest, Goety, and The Aether (on top of Mowzie's, Ice and Fire, and Born in Chaos).
- **New natural attack types** — poison bite, freeze zone, lightning strike, charge, and sonic blast, with defaults for signature modded mobs.
- **Morph shape persistence** — variant / NBT shape data (e.g. Cobblemon species) is stored and restored so morphs no longer flicker between forms.
- **Sovereign Amulet** — normal click fills a vial; shift-click grants a morph orb unlock for late-game instant capture.
- **Survival as…** — proper Create World game mode with mob picker, locked morph identity, ×20 slower knowledge XP, hard diet, mild circadian, Habit Chrysalis, and a first-join traits popup (icons + prey/creature lists).

### Fixes

- **Echo Forge** — block is mineable again (iron pickaxe+) and drops its inventory when broken.
- **Forge 1.20.1** — Echo Forge inventory drop uses the correct item handler API.
- **Survival as…** — Remorphed no longer steals the traits popup; morph knowledge XP actually accumulates; quadruped mouth/hotbar cascades items from locked slots; NeoForge 1.21.8 join/UI/lock polish (no Morph Binding potion).

**Requires:** CraftedCore, Walkers, ReMorphed.
