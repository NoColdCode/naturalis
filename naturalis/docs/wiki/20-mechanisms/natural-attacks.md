# Natural Attacks

Natural Attacks replace normal punching while morphed and unarmed.

## Trigger rules

Natural Attack triggers when:
- You are morphed
- You attack a living target
- Your main hand is not a normal tool/weapon class

If attack is on cooldown:
- Hit is blocked
- You get a cooldown action-bar message

## Guardian beam defaults

Guardian:
- Damage: `5`
- Range: `18`
- Cooldown: `32 ticks` (`1.6 s`)

Elder Guardian:
- Damage: `8`
- Range: `24`
- Cooldown: `36 ticks` (`1.8 s`)

## Attack families

- Fireball / Small Fireball
- Arrow
- Dash bite
- Leap attack
- Enhanced melee

## Configurable values

Per morph profile, Naturalis can tune:
- Attack type
- Damage
- Range
- Velocity
- Cooldown
- Replace normal attack toggle

## Practical use

- Keep one hand free to use morph combat identity.
- Time your attacks around cooldown windows.
- Use ranged morphs to open fights, mobility morphs to finish.
