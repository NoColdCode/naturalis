# Diet

Diet depends on your current morph body.

## Diet types

- Carnivore
- Herbivore
- Omnivore

## Wrong-food penalty roll

When you eat food outside your morph diet, one result is rolled:
- `12%`: Poison (`120 ticks` / `6 s`)
- `28%`: Hunger (`220 ticks` / `11 s`)
- `28%`: Nausea (`180 ticks` / `9 s`)
- `32%`: no extra status effect

## Resonance humanity interaction

In human form while Resonance is active:

At humanity `<=80`:
- Wrong-diet eating starts adding extra nutrition/exhaustion penalties.

At humanity `<=60`:
- Wrong-diet penalties intensify.

At humanity `<=40`:
- Wrong-diet food is blocked completely.

## Token reminder

Humanity Tokens can help stabilize this loop before diet penalties become hard locks.
