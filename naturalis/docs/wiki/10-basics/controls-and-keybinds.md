# Controls and Keybinds

These are the most important default controls for Naturalis.

## Core keys

- Open Morph Knowledge: shows your current morph progress and branch info.
- Resonance Instinct key: triggers Resonance Active Instinct (if available).
- Primal Movement (default `G`): triggers movement-specific actions for compatible morphs.

## Active Instinct usage checks

Active Instinct only works when:
- Resonance is enabled
- You are in your bonded morph
- Cooldown is finished

Cooldown values depend on humanity and mastery:
- Base `45/38/32/26/20 s` by humanity stage
- `-2 s` per mastery tier
- Minimum `12 s`

## Species communication call

Call behavior (hold Shift):
- Searches same-species allies in `24 blocks`
- Requires at least 1 ally within `12 blocks` to trigger
- Rally cap per call: up to `20 allies`
- Cooldown:
  - Social rank 0-1: `60 ticks` (`3 s`)
  - Social rank 2+: `30 ticks` (`1.5 s`)

## Quality-of-life tips

- Keep at least one free hand for Natural Attacks.
- Use Morph Knowledge screen often when planning a main morph.
- At low humanity, avoid panic key-spam and stabilize first.
