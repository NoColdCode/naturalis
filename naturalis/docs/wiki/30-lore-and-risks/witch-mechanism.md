# Witch Mechanism

Witches and evokers are major "identity disruption" enemies in Naturalis.

## Witch binding pressure

When a witch (or witch projectile) damages you:
- You can receive Morph Binding
- Binding duration roll: `5` to `30 seconds`
- Re-application cooldown on you: `60 ticks` (`3 s`)

This means one witch can lock your morph rhythm if you stay in line of fire.

## Witch/evoker transmutation gifts

When damaged by an aggro witch/evoker, a transmutation gift can trigger.

Witch values:
- Gift chance: `10%`
- Internal cooldown: `14 s`
- Typical gift transform duration: around `70 s`

Evoker values:
- Gift chance: `22%`
- Internal cooldown: `9 s`
- Typical gift transform duration: around `90 s`

## Coven Sigil Key drops

Witch or evoker kill drop chance:
- `45%` chance to drop Coven Sigil Key

Coven Sigil Key is the main key item used to activate Natural Portal frames.

## Combat meaning

If you are running Resonance and low humanity, witches become more dangerous than raw-damage mobs because they disrupt form control and recovery timing.
