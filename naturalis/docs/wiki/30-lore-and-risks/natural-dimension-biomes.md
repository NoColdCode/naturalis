# Natural Dimension Biomes

Naturalis Natural Dimension includes 13 signature biome identities.

## Biome list

- Natural Plain
- Natural Beach
- Dense Forest
- Jungle Real
- Arid Savanna
- High Peak
- Snowy Mountain
- Volcano
- Deep Water
- Coral Water
- Dark Caves
- Ender Forest
- Natural Echo

## High-risk highlights

Volcano:
- Hazardous heat style environment
- Special fire interactions exist for some undead entities in this biome

Natural Echo:
- Central high-danger identity zone
- Echo Sovereign ecosystem anchors here

Deep Water / Coral Water:
- Aquatic suitability becomes very important

Dark Caves / Ender Forest:
- Strong pressure zones for unprepared morph profiles

## Why this matters

Natural Dimension is not cosmetic worldgen.
Your morph suitability and humanity management determine whether these biomes feel like opportunities or punishment.
