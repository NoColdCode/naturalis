# Items: Resonance and Survival

## Humanity and stability items

- Memory Token (`+15 humanity`)
- Humanity Token 5 (`+5 humanity`)
- Humanity Token 10 (`+10 humanity`)
- Grounding Focus

## Signature dimension and identity items

- Coven Sigil Key
- Human Amulet
- Natural Star
- Sovereign Amulet

## Important interactions

Coven Sigil Key:
- Used to activate Natural Portal frames
- Common source: witch/evoker drops (`45%` chance)

Human Amulet:
- Improves positive humanity recovery gain while in human form

Sovereign Amulet:
- Prevents negative humanity effects from dropping you below `1` in its protection cases

Natural Star:
- Rare high-value material item
- Warden drop in Natural Dimension: `2.5%`
