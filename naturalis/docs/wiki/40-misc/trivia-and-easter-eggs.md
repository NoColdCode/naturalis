# Trivia and Easter Eggs

## Lightning lock easter egg

If lightning strikes you while morphed:
- You receive Morph Binding with effectively infinite duration until cleared by gameplay state changes.

## Humanoid perception exception

Even though most morphs use altered visual filters, humanoid-style forms (villager/witch/illager family) keep normal vision.

## Hidden social detail

Species call does nothing if no ally is close enough (`12 blocks`) even if allies exist farther away inside the wider `24` block scan.

## Why signs changed

Naturalis previously mutated sign readability directly. That heavy method was removed and replaced with lightweight action-bar comprehension cues.

## Atmospheric trick

Morphed music volume reduction is subtle at high humanity (`0.90x`) and becomes dramatic only near identity collapse (`0.35x`).

## Portal ritual flavor

Coven Sigil Key is intentionally tied to witch/evoker kills, so entering the Natural Dimension feels like a ritual earned through hostile magic encounters.
